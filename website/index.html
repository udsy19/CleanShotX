Privacy

Your screen stays yours

Super Screenshot is a local app. Captures, text recognition, stitching and video encoding all happen on-device. There's no account and no analytics, and nothing is uploaded until you set up a destination of your own.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reports
  • Uploads only to storage you own
  • Upload keys kept in your Keychain

Opening the DMG instead? Super Screenshot isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS asks once: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. The one-line installer skips this step.
