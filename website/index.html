Screenshots and screen recordings for the Mac.

A small menu bar app that captures, marks up and records your screen. Free and open source, for macOS 14 and later.

Captures stay on your Mac.

No account and no analytics. Text recognition, stitching and encoding run on your Mac. MIT licensed. Super Screenshot updates itself from GitHub releases; you can turn that off in Settings → General.

Free for macOS 14 and later.
