1.3.2
Crash-safe recordings
October 19, 2026

Recordings are written in fragments, so a crash, a force quit or a power cut keeps everything up to the last two seconds. Annotate keeps a recovery copy of unsaved edits.

1.3.1
First automatic update
October 18, 2026

A maintenance release, and the first one delivered quietly in the background by the new updater.

1.3.0
Automatic updates
October 17, 2026

New versions download in the background, are checked against their SHA-256 and code signature, and install when you quit. You can turn it off in Settings.

1.2.0
A new name
October 17, 2026

Shotcraft is now Super Screenshot. Settings and captures move over on their own, and projects saved under the old name still open.

1.1.0
Quick Access, refined
October 17, 2026

Cards spring in from the edge of the screen and slide away when closed, with centred Copy and Save buttons and a five-step size slider.

A new icon
October 17, 2026

An ink viewfinder with a red capture dot on warm paper, and a "Private by design" step in onboarding.

First public release

Download for Mac

Prefer the Terminal?

One line downloads the latest release, installs it in Applications and opens it.

Run it again to update, or add -s -- --uninstall to remove the app. The DMG isn't notarized, so its first launch needs System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
